== 1. THE ENGINE (5 Oct 2026): the reference ==
Auto-battle in the real MZ engine (headless Chromium), tools/scen_story_bal2.js, on the installed data: the party is
built as in section 2 (same stat policies, breakthroughs at the story's walls, Rin in the Iron Prince fight, Yukino
from chapter 7, regalia as you collect them). 12-20 fights per line, so a line is +-10-15 points of noise.
"tuned" = the factors of tools/story/engine_tune.py (offence = STR, DEX and MAG, DEX also sets the foe's AC; HP where
noted), found by bisection in the engine with tools/scen_tune.js.
Targets (your direction: regular encounters fairly difficult, bosses very difficult): regular fights >= 90% with
roughly half the party's HP gone; mid-bosses 60-75%; bosses 45-60% for auto-battle. A player who uses items
(antidotes against poison), guards, support skills and focus fire does better than auto-battle.
Why the engine differs from section 2: the simulator doesn't model states and buffs applied by skills (poison takes
10% of max HP every turn until the fight ends; the Sirene's sleep song; fear, webs, agility debuffs, Heerruf), the
Seelenkessel healing itself from what Seelenstrom drains (four people, twice a turn), or full-rate multi-target
bites. Shirogane yields at 40% HP and Kurenai Tōma's battle ends at turn 2 (story pages; counted as wins).
The final battle is two fights in a row with a full heal between them: Kagerō, then Hōkai's shadow.
Columns: level, party rank, kind, troop, win rate, rounds, average party HP left after a win.
