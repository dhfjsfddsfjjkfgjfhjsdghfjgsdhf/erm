## 4. Balance (what the user asked for, and what I did)

The user's words, mid-session: *"make regular encounters not too easy, and make bosses quite strong"*, then
*"keep regular encounters fairly difficult, while bosses should feel very difficult"*.

`tools/battle_sim.py` ports the rules of Rank_Core/Rank_Battle/Story_Core (d20 vs AC, dice + POW + 10×rank, the
rank-gap multiplier, weakness, saves, crits, multi-target rate, minion rate, role extra actions, enemy action
ratings, MP costs, skill forms, passives, floating strikes, riposte, regen, Unbroken, Hold the Line, the regalia
and the Veil of Ash) and fights with MZ's auto-battle choice. It builds the party like `scen_story_bal2.js`
(same stat policies). Against your engine numbers for chapter 4 it lands close on regular fights and about
20 points harsher on bosses (Messingvogt: 66% here, 88% in your engine run).

Before tuning, Act II–III regular fights took 2 rounds and cost ~10% HP, and nearly every boss was unwinnable
(0% in 40 rounds): bosses sat a rank above the party (×½ damage dealt, ×1½ taken, plus a boss action) with
HP pools of 9,000–21,000. `tools/tune_foes.py` then set, per enemy, HP (how long a fight lasts) and an offence
factor on STR/DEX/INT/MAG (how much it hurts), against these targets in the sim:

| Kind | Rounds | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Regular encounter | 4–7 | wins ≥ 97%, party left with ~40–60% HP |
| Mid-boss / elite | 7–10 | wins ~55–75% |
| Boss | 10–15 | wins ~35–50%, survivors on ~15–30% HP |

Chapters 5–6 also reused four Chapter 4 troops (hellhounds, gargoyles, ghouls) that the party now killed in two
rounds; they use three new variants instead (enemies 123 Heereshund, 124 Heeresgargyl, 125 Grabghul: the Act I–II
art with a hue shift), tuned like the rest. Chapter 4's own enemies are untouched.

Rank changes that came with it (EXP raised ×1.5 on these so the level curve doesn't slow down): Aschenschmied,
Der Eiserne Prinz, Messingsoldat, Oni-Söldner, Oni-Hauptmann, Seelenkessel C→D; Arenabestie, Klingenmeisterin,
Kampfmagier, Schattenklinge, Kanemoto Gōki B→C; Fomorer-Häuptling A→B. Grubenwicht, Hohler Diener and Irrlicht
are no longer minions (minions deal half damage, which kept those swarms trivial). The final numbers are in
`tools/story/foes.py` and `foes3.py`, and the table the sim printed is in `handoff/balance.txt`.

Please confirm in the engine. If the engine is kinder than the sim by the same ~20 points, bosses end up around
55–70% for auto-battle, which a player who uses support skills, barriers and items will beat, but not easily.
