## Balance: the engine disagrees with battle_sim.py
The simulator doesn't model states and buffs applied by skills (poison takes 10% of max HP every turn until the fight
ends; the Sirene's sleep song; fear, webs, agility debuffs, Heerruf), the Seelenkessel's drain healing it for what
it takes from four people twice a turn, or skills that hit several random targets at full rate. In the engine
(auto-battle over every troop of chapters 5–9 at the story's levels) ten bosses and mid-bosses were walls (0–25%
wins) and six regular fights won only 25–75%.

`tools/story/engine_tune.py` now scales the offence (STR, DEX and MAG; DEX is also their AC) of 18 foes and the HP of
three after the database is built. Every factor was found by bisection in the engine (`tools/scen_tune.js`) and the
affected troops were run again on the final data. Result (auto-battle): regular fights 92–100% with 33–74% HP left,
mid-bosses 50–85% (Shirogane yields by design), bosses 45–65% in 10–17 rounds; the final battle is Kagerō 70% then
Hōkai's shadow 35% (healed in between). Table: top of `handoff/balance.txt`. If you retune with the simulator,
re-check those foes in the engine.
